Each word becomes a list of numbers, one per dimension. Here we pretend dimension 1 measures how noun-like a word is and dimension 2 how human-related it is. “talks” is a verb that humans do, so it sits top left. “bark” is a verb dogs do, so it sits bottom left. “toy” is a noun used by both kids and pets, so it sits right and near the middle vertically. “learning” can be a verb or a noun, so it sits close to the middle on that axis. “dog” and “cat” are both animal nouns, so they land close together in the bottom right, near each other and far from “talks”. In a real model the dimensions are learned, not named, and there are hundreds of them. That is what the training demo shows next.
