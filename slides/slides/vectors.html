Word embeddings

Each word is an arrow from the origin

Words with similar meanings point in similar directions. The smaller the angle, the more alike they are.

PairAngleCosine similarity
cat · dog17°+0.95

Cosine similarity is the cosine of the angle: +1 means the same direction, −1 means opposite.

Now draw each word as an arrow from the origin to its point. Dog and cat point almost the same way: only 17 degrees apart, so their cosine similarity is about 0.95. Compare dog with talks: they point in opposite directions, so the similarity is about −1. This is how a model measures how alike two words are. It doesn’t look at the letters at all, only at the direction of the vectors. In the training demo, watch the cat and dog arrows swing toward each other.
